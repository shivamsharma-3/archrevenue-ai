Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

Is RevScout a CRM replacement?

No. RevScout works alongside your existing CRM. The CRM stores your records; RevScout monitors live commercial signals to tell you who to contact today and what to say.

How does RevScout find buying signals?

The autonomous engine continuously tracks publicly accessible hiring postings, news, funding announcements, and technology changes, comparing them against established baselines.

How is RevScout different from lead scrapers like Apollo or ZoomInfo?

Scrapers only give you static contact lists. RevScout provides strategic timing and messaging: which accounts in your pipeline have active commercial momentum and the exact angle of approach.
